# Handmade Volume Fan Engineering 

## From Classic to Multi-Fiber Applications 

While a classic extension service involves placing one extension onto one natural lash, volume lashing requires an artist to create "fans." A volume fan consists of 2 to 6 microscopic extension fibers grouped together, flared out at the top, and fused together at a microscopic, singular base. This allows the artist to build density and dramatic texture for clients with sparse natural lashes. 
### Core Hand Techniques for Fan Creation 

Creating symmetrical handmade fans requires hours of muscle memory development. Artists generally master one of three primary manual manipulation techniques: 

1. **The Pinching Method:** Pull the desired number of fibers off the sticky strip, place them between your thumb and index finger, and gently pinch the base to flare the tips. 
2. **The Lonely Fan Method:** Pull fibers forward on the strip, isolate them from the rest, and roll the tweezer tip sideways to separate the tops before picking them up. 
3. **The Shimmy Technique:** Grip the extensions directly on the strip and apply minute horizontal vibrations with your wrist to pop the fan open.

## Micro-Base Mechanics and Weight Limits 

The base of a volume fan must be tightly wrapped and completely seamless. A boxy, split, or thick base will cause immediate irritation and poor retention. Technicians must strictly balance their fan sizes according to the hair thickness guidelines outlined in [[products-and-tools/lash-extension-types|lash extension fiber types]], ensuring a 5D fan uses ultra-light 0.03mm or 0.05mm diameters to preserve natural hair health.

# Mechanical Isolation Protocols 

## The Foundation of Safe Lash Application

Perfect mechanical isolation is the non-negotiable hallmark of a professional lash service. Isolation refers to the physical separation of a single, natural hair away from all surrounding lashes on the eyelid margin. If an extension is glued to more than one natural hair, it creates a "sticky." As the hair grows at different rates, stickies cause acute follicular pain, permanent root damage, and premature shedding. 

### Step-by-Step Isolation Mechanics 

Achieving clean isolation requires steady fine-motor control and an optimized physical workspace: 
* **The Anchor:** Place your pinky and ring fingers gently on your client's forehead or eye pad to stabilize your non-dominant isolation hand. 
* **The Entry:** Insert your straight or slightly curved isolation tweezers downward into the lash line at a 45-degree angle. 
* **The Separation:** Gently spread the tweezers apart to isolate a single hair, using the tips to hold neighboring hairs back out of the zone. 

> **Pro-Tip:** If a client has an incredibly dense natural lash line, isolate from the bottom layer upward by taping up the top layers of lashes with medical tape.

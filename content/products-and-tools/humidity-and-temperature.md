# Humidity and Temperature Control 

## The Delicate Environmental Balance

Eyelash extension adhesives are incredibly sensitive to their environment. Managing room temperature and relative humidity (RH) is the single most important factor in achieving clean isolation and strong bond retention. 
### Ideal Workspace Parameters

Most professional cyanoacrylate glues perform best within a narrow window. Technicians should aim to maintain the following baseline metrics: 
* **Relative Humidity:** Between 45% and 55% RH
* **Ambient Room Temperature:** Between 68℉ and 72℉ (20℃ and 22℃)
* **Air Circulation:** Low, draft-free ventilation to prevent premature curing on the stone or in the glue ring 
## Troubleshooting Environmental Extremes

When humidity spikes, the adhesive cures almost instantly upon exposure to air, resulting in "shock curing." This manifests as white, brittle frost on the glue bond, compromising the structural integrity of the application. Conversely, low humidity starves the glue of moisture, causing extensions to slip off during brushing. If your environment fluctuates wildly, it renders even the highest quality [[tweezer-ergonomics|precision tweezers]] ineffective due to slipping bonds. Always store your open bottles inside a sealed [[adhesive-storage|airtight storage container]] to shield them from ambient air fluctuations.

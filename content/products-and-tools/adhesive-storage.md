# Professional Adhesive Storage Protocols

## Preserving Chemical Longevity

An open bottle of standard lash glue degrades rapidly if exposed to light, heat, and moisture fluctuations. To maximize shelf life and ensure predictable behavior during client applications, proper daily maintenance and storage protocols are non-negotiable. 

### Unopened vs. Opened Bottles

Unopened bottles can be stored safely in a dark refrigerator for up to three months to slow the breakdown of stabilizers. However, once a bottle is unsealed, it must never re-enter the refrigerator. The sudden temperature shifts cause internal condensation, triggering premature polymerization inside the bottle. 

## Daily Storage Best Practices 

Always store open bottles upright inside a dedicated vacuum-sealed container packed with silica beads or dry uncooked rice to draw out moisture. Wipe the nozzle thoroughly with a lint-free wipe after every pour to prevent the cap from gluing shut. Maintaining fresh chemistry through storage ensures predictable performance when working inside your [[humidity-and-temperature|optimal humidity parameters]] to create lasting client bonds.

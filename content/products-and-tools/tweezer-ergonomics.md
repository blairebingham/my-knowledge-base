# Tweezer Ergonomics and Precision Selection

## The Extension of the Artist's Hand

Tweezers are the primary physical tools of a lash professional. Because an artist spends up to eight hours a day applying micro-weight fibers, selecting tools with the proper weight, tension, and alignment is critical for preventing repetitive strain injuries like carpal tunnel syndrome. 

### Isolation vs. Placement Tweezers

An extension service requires two distinct tool profiles acting in tandem. The isolation hand typically utilizes a straight or slightly curved tweezer to separate a single natural lash away from its neighbors. The dominant hand utilizes an angled, boot, or L-shaped tweezer to grip, manipulate, and place extensions seamlessly.

## Finding the Sweet Spot

Every handmade tweezer possesses a unique "sweet spot" - the exact point along the physical tip where maximum closing tension occurs. If a tweezer has a loose sweet spot, delicate volume fans will slip and collapse during pickup. Technicians must routinely test their tools to maintain pristine alignment.

# Automated Booking and Client Management Systems

## Streamlining Salon Operations via Software 

As an independent lash practitioner or salon owner, your time is your single most valuable asset. Spending hours managing text messages, manual back-and-forth scheduling, and late appointment cancellations dramatically cuts into your active revenue-producing hours. Implementing a modern, automated cloud-based booking and client management system is essential for protecting your schedule and scaling your revenue.

### Critical Features of a Salon Management System 

Look for a dedicated beauty industry software platform (such as GlossGenius, Acuity, or Vagaro) that automates these operational tasks:

* **Online Self-Scheduling:** Allows clients to view your live availability, book appointments, and securely input a credit card to hold their time slot 24/7. 
* **Automated Deposit and Cancellation Protection:** Charges a non-refundable deposit at booking to eliminate financial losses from last-minute "no-shows." 
* **Digital Client Profiles (CRM):** Stores specific historical records for each client, including custom lash maps and notes on their preferred styling curl profiles.

## Synchronizing Intake Workflows 

Your automated booking system should serve as your primary intake gateway. Program your platform to automatically email necessary screening documents and legal liability waivers to clients 24 hours before their arrival. This automation ensures all steps detailed in our [[client-consultation-and-care/patch-testing-protocols|patch testing and client intake protocols]] are legally signed and documented before the client ever lays down on your table.

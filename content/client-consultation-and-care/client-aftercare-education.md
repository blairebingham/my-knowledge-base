# Long-Term Client Aftercare Education

## Empowering the Client for Maximum Retention

While a lash artist is completely responsible for clean isolation and precise chemical bonds during the appointment, the client is responsible for maintaining that work once they leave the studio. Educating your clients on proper aftercare protocols is the single most effective way to eliminate premature shedding and maintain structural hygiene between fill appointments.

### Mandatory Daily Maintenance Guidelines 

Provide every client with a physical or digital aftercare card detailing these strict lifestyle rules: 

* **The Cleaining Rule:** Wash the extensions daily using a dedicated, oil-free lash foam cleanser. Leaving natural oils to accumulate will break down the adhesive bond. 
* **The Mechanical Rule:** Brush the lashes gently with a clean mascara wand only when they are completely dry. Never pull, pick, or twist tangles. 
* **The Product Rule:** Eliminate all oil-based makeup removers, cream eye shadows, and waterproof mascaras from your daily beauty routine.

## Hygiene and Health Implications 

Failing to wash extensions out of fear that they will fall off is a dangerous misconception. Neglecting daily cleansing allows dead skin cells and sebum to build up along the lid margin. This neglect creates a breeding ground for severe bacterial infections and inflammatory conditions detailed in our overview of [[eyelash-anatomy-and-health/ocular-conditions|ocular conditions and contraindications]].

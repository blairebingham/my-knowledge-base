# Patch Testing and Client Intake Protocols

## Setting Legal Boundaries and Managing Expectations 

The initial client consultation is your primary opportunity to review medical histories, establish client expectations, and shield your business from legal liability using formalized intake waivers. This process ensures that clients understand the chemistry behind the service and consent to the application before any products are used.

### The Anatomy of an Effective Intake Process 

Every new client file must systematically compile the following documentation before the application begins:

1. **Medical History Review:** Explicitly check for a history of eye surgeries, alopecia, or thyroid conditions that directly impact natural hair shedding. 
2. **The Patch Test:** Apply 5 to 10 short extension fibers to the outer corners of each eye using your standard adhesive. Wait 24 to 48 hours to monitor the skin for low-grade inflammation. 
3. **Liability Waiver:** A signed legal document confirming the client understands the risks of chemical sensitivities and agrees to follow mandatory hygiene guidelines.

## Screening for Chemical Sensitivities 

The primary goal of a patch test is to screen for localized contact dermatitis. True allergic reactions are almost always triggered by the black pigment or the fast-curing vapors analyzed in our [[products-and-tools/adhesive-chemistry|lash adhesive chemistry guide]]. If a client reacts during the monitoring window, the full service must be canceled immediately.

# Ocular Conditions & Contraindications

## Identifying Medical Risks in the Lash Suite 

As a non-medical beauty professional, a lash artist must be legally trained to identify visible eye conditions that present immediate contraindications to a lash extension service. A contraindication is any medical condition, physical attribute, or skin irritation that makes an extension service unsafe to perform. 

### Common Contraindications & Sensitivities 

If a client presents with any of the following symptoms or conditions during their initial intake, the service must be politely declined or postponed until cleared by a physician: 

1. **Conjunctivitis (Pink Eye):** A highly contagious viral or bacterial infection of the conjunctiva membrane characterized by deep redness and discharge. 
2. **Contact Dermatitis:** An acute allergic reaction or irritation of the eyelid skin. This condition can easily be triggered if a client develops a sensitivity to carbon black or cyanoacrylate vapors highlighted in our [[02-products-and-tools/adhesive-chemistry|adhesive chemistry analysis]]. 
3. **Blepharitis:** Chronic inflammation of the eyelid margins, often caused by poor hygiene, an overgrowth of bacteria, or Demodex eyelash mites.

> "When in doubt, refer them out. Never jeopardize a client's ocular vision or health for an aesthetic enhancement service."

## Minimizing Ocular Sensitivities

To ensure your workplace minimizes these risks, implement strict sanitation protocols before any appointment. Proper tool sterilization detailed in our [[04-safety-and-sanitization/index|sanitization standards guide]] remains your primary shield against cross-contamination and client eye infections.
